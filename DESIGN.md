# Desktop interface

Visual thesis: a quiet petroleum-study desk with petrol-blue actions, pale neutral surfaces, strong reading hierarchy, and very little chrome.

Content plan: the Study workspace leads with the due queue and one study action; the card owns recall and answer explanation; a secondary column holds topic filters and the daily plan. Bank, Quizzes, Progress, and Guide each have one operational purpose.

Interaction thesis: brief answer reveals orient the eye; restrained hover changes signal actions; dialogs enter gently and respect reduced motion. Keyboard study uses Space to reveal and 1–4 to rate after reveal. Focus indicators remain visible. No network resources or web fonts are required.
